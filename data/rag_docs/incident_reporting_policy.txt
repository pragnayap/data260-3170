BAYWOOD TRANSIT AUTHORITY
Incident Reporting Policy (Policy No. BTA-OPS-101)
Effective 1 January 2025

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes how field staff, operators, and dispatchers at Baywood
Transit Authority (BTA) must record and escalate incidents that occur on any
bus, light rail, or express route the Authority operates. It applies to every
employee, contractor, and station agent who observes or responds to an
incident while on duty.

2. REQUIRED FIELDS

Every incident report filed in the Authority's incident system must include:
the routeId of the vehicle or service involved (the primary identifying
field), the location where the incident occurred (the secondary field,
recorded as a cross street, station name, or milepost), the submitterEmail
of the employee filing the report, and a free-text description of at least
25 characters. Reports missing any of these four fields are rejected by the
intake system and returned to the submitter for correction.

3. INCIDENT CATEGORIES

Every incident must be classified into exactly one of four categories:
Delay, Mechanical Failure, Accident-Collision, or Safety Hazard. The
category determines which downstream office reviews the report and how
quickly it must be escalated. Field staff select the category at the time
of filing; the Safety Office may recategorize a report during review if the
initial classification was incorrect, but the original category is retained
in the record for audit purposes.

4. FILING WINDOW

Field staff must file the initial incident report within 30 minutes of the
incident occurring, using the mobile incident app or, if unavailable, a
radio call to dispatch followed by a written report before the end of the
shift.

5. ESCALATION TO THE SAFETY OFFICE

Once a report has been filed, reports categorized as Accident-Collision or
Safety Hazard must be escalated to the Safety Office within 2 hours of the
report being filed in the system. This 2-hour clock starts when the report
is submitted, not when the incident occurred, and applies regardless of
severity. Delay and Mechanical Failure reports do not carry this 2-hour
escalation requirement; they are reviewed during the Safety Office's normal
weekly batch review instead, unless a Mechanical Failure report is also
flagged Critical under the hazard severity procedure, in which case the
hazard procedure's timeline governs instead.

6. RECORD RETENTION

Completed incident records, including all attachments and the escalation
history, are retained for 5 years from the date of filing. After 5 years,
records are archived to cold storage and are no longer editable, though they
remain available to auditors on request.

7. AMENDMENTS

Any amendment to a filed report must be logged with the amending employee's
submitterEmail and a timestamp. The original submitted version is never
overwritten; amendments are stored as a new version linked to the original
report.
