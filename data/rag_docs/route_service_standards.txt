BAYWOOD TRANSIT AUTHORITY
Route and Service Standards (Standard No. BTA-SVC-200)
Revised March 2025

1. ROUTE IDENTIFIER SCHEME

Every route operated by Baywood Transit Authority is assigned a routeId in
the form BTA-XYY, where the leading digit indicates the mode of service.
Routes numbered BTA-1xx are local bus routes. Routes numbered BTA-2xx are
light rail lines. Routes numbered BTA-3xx are limited-stop express bus
routes that run only during peak commute hours. This numbering scheme is
fixed; a routeId is retired, not reused, if a route is permanently
discontinued.

2. ON-TIME PERFORMANCE TARGET

The Authority's system-wide on-time performance target is that at least 90
percent of scheduled trips must arrive within 5 minutes of their scheduled
time at each timepoint along the route. On-time performance is measured
monthly per route and published in the Authority's quarterly service
report.

3. DELAY LOGGING THRESHOLD

Any single trip that arrives more than 10 minutes later than its scheduled
time must be logged as an incident under the Delay category, per the
Incident Reporting Policy. Delays of 10 minutes or less are tracked in the
scheduling system but do not require an individual incident report.

4. MISSED TRIPS AND SERVICE REVIEW

If a route experiences 3 consecutive missed trips within a single service
day, the route is automatically flagged for a service review by the
Service Planning division. A service review examines whether the published
schedule, vehicle assignment, or operator staffing for that route needs to
be adjusted. Service reviews are completed within 15 business days of being
triggered.

5. VEHICLE ASSIGNMENT

Light rail routes (BTA-2xx) are assigned only to rail vehicles that have
passed the wheel and axle inspection described in the Authority's mechanical
maintenance standard within the preceding 12,000 miles of operation. Bus
routes (BTA-1xx and BTA-3xx) are assigned buses that have passed a current
brake inspection.

6. HOLIDAY AND REDUCED SERVICE

On the six designated holidays each year, all BTA-1xx and BTA-3xx routes
operate on a reduced Sunday schedule. BTA-2xx light rail lines continue to
operate on their normal weekday schedule on holidays, except for the
December 25 service suspension used for scheduled system-wide track
maintenance.
