BAYWOOD TRANSIT AUTHORITY
Accident and Collision Investigation Procedures (Procedure No. BTA-SAF-320)
Effective 1 January 2025

1. SCOPE

This procedure applies to every incident classified under the
Accident-Collision category: a collision between a BTA vehicle and another
vehicle, a pedestrian, a cyclist, another BTA vehicle, or a fixed object,
as well as any onboard accident that causes a passenger or employee injury
without an external collision (for example, a fall caused by hard braking).

2. IMMEDIATE NOTIFICATION FOR INJURY COLLISIONS

Any Accident-Collision incident that results in an injury to a passenger,
employee, or third party must be reported to the Safety Office within 2
hours of the incident occurring. This 2-hour window is measured from the
moment of the collision itself, not from when the written report is later
filed, which distinguishes it from the report-filing-based clock used for
non-injury Accident-Collision and Safety Hazard reports under the Incident
Reporting Policy.

3. EXTERNAL OVERSIGHT NOTIFICATION FOR FATALITIES

If an Accident-Collision incident results in a fatality, the Safety Office
must additionally notify the State Transit Safety Oversight body within 4
hours of the incident occurring, separate from and in addition to the
2-hour internal Safety Office notification described above.

4. INVESTIGATION AND PROBABLE CAUSE

Every Accident-Collision incident is assigned an investigator from the
Safety Office. The investigator must determine a probable cause and issue a
final investigation report within 30 days of the incident. Each
investigation classifies the collision as either Preventable, meaning BTA
operator action or vehicle condition contributed to the cause, or
Non-preventable, meaning the cause originated entirely outside BTA's
control, such as a third-party driver running a red light.

5. VEHICLE HOLD DURING INVESTIGATION

A vehicle involved in an Accident-Collision incident that results in injury
or a fatality is held out of revenue service until the assigned investigator
completes an initial inspection, which must occur within 24 hours of the
incident. The vehicle may be returned to service after that inspection
unless mechanical involvement is suspected, in which case it remains held
until the maintenance standard's inspection requirements are also
satisfied.

6. DRIVER AND OPERATOR STATEMENTS

Any BTA operator involved in an Accident-Collision incident must provide a
written statement to the assigned investigator within 48 hours of the
incident, separate from the initial incident report filed under the
Incident Reporting Policy.
